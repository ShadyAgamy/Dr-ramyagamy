A semen analysis... one of the most important tests.

## The husband's role in delayed pregnancy

About **40%** of the causes of delayed pregnancy come from the husband. The cause may be:

- A low sperm count.
- Weak motility.
- Abnormal sperm shape.

A semen analysis must be done accurately, in a reliable lab. And simple treatments can make a big difference.

It's not all on the wife.

## Low sperm motility

Low motility means the sperm don't reach the egg easily.

**Possible causes:**

- Varicocele.
- Poor nutrition.
- Infections.

**Treatment:** we start with tests, then medication, and sometimes a minor procedure. In some cases we turn to ICSI.

If your doctor told you that you have low motility, don't delay.
