The difference between IUI and ICSI isn't only the cost.

## What is IUI (intrauterine insemination)?

In IUI, we prepare the sperm and place them in the uterus at the time of ovulation.

## When do we use IUI?

- When the husband has a low sperm count or slightly weak motility.
- When the wife ovulates well, but pregnancy is delayed with no clear cause.

The procedure is simple and not painful, but it needs good preparation.

## The difference between IUI and ICSI

- **IUI:** the sperm enter the uterus and complete their journey on their own.
- **ICSI:** we choose the sperm ourselves and inject it into the egg under the microscope.

We turn to ICSI in more difficult cases, or when IUI has failed.
