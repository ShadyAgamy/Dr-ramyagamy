Feeling pain on one side? It might be ovulation... but not always.

## Signs of ovulation

The signs of ovulation aren't always clear. Some women notice:

- Mild pain on one side.
- Clear discharge that looks like egg white.
- A slight rise in body temperature.

But this doesn't mean there is a mature egg ready for pregnancy.

## The best way to confirm ovulation

**A follicle-tracking ultrasound in the middle of the cycle.** It shows us the size of the egg and whether there is a real chance of pregnancy.

## What is weak ovulation?

Weak ovulation means the egg doesn't grow to the required size, or isn't released at all.

### Causes

- Polycystic ovaries (PCOS).
- Hormonal imbalance.
- Excess weight.

### Treatment

Thankfully there are many treatments, such as stimulating ovulation with medication or simple injections. But follow-up must be done with ultrasound, so that we don't overdo the stimulation or delay treatment.
