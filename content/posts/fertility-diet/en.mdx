Can food help you get pregnant? Definitely.

Diet plays a big role in improving fertility in both women and men.

## Helpful foods

- Vegetables.
- Fruit.
- Nuts.
- Fish.
- Olive oil.

## What to cut down on

- Sugar.
- Trans fats.
- Fast food.

## Vitamins

Vitamins such as **folic acid** are very important before pregnancy.

Every bite counts... choose well.
