Two lines on the test? Do you have to see the doctor right away? Not always.

## When should the first visit be?

When a urine or blood test comes back positive, you don't need to go the same day.

We wait **2 to 3 weeks** so that we can see the pregnancy sac on the ultrasound.

But if there is **severe cramping or bleeding**... go right away.

## Normal pregnancy signs

Nausea, tiredness, dizziness... these are normal signs of pregnancy.

## Signs that need an urgent check

- Continuous bleeding.
- Severe pain on one side.
- A high fever.

Every body is different, but some things shouldn't be ignored.

## Ectopic pregnancy

An ectopic pregnancy means the embryo has implanted in a fallopian tube, or somewhere other than the uterus.

**Its signs:**

- Severe pain on one side.
- A late period.
- An abnormal pregnancy test.

A transvaginal ultrasound confirms it quickly. It needs prompt treatment, because it is dangerous if delayed.

Your safety comes first... see your doctor as soon as you suspect something.
