At the clinics of Dr. Ramy Agamy (Consultant of Obstetrics, Gynecology and ICSI), we are committed to protecting the privacy of all patients, website visitors and followers of our social media pages.

## Information we collect

Appointments are booked on WhatsApp or by phone. When you contact us, we receive only what you share, such as:

- Your name
- Your phone number
- The clinic and day that suit you

**Please do not send any medical details in booking messages.** Your case is discussed with the doctor directly.

## How we use your information

Your information is used only to:

- Answer your questions
- Book appointments and confirm them by phone or WhatsApp
- Improve the quality of our service

## Data protection

We keep all data confidential and do not share it with any third party for marketing, except where required by law.

## Third-party services used by this website

- **WhatsApp:** the WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp. The conversation is covered by WhatsApp's privacy policy.
- **Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics:** we may use Google tools to see how many people visit and which pages are most viewed, in general and without identifying you. These tools may use cookies.
- **Hosting:** the website is hosted on Netlify, which may keep technical logs such as IP addresses for security.

## Medical content

Content published on this website and on our pages is for awareness only and is not a substitute for a direct medical consultation.
