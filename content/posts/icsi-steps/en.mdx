From the first day of the cycle to embryo transfer, this is the ICSI journey.

## The steps of ICSI

1. **Ovarian stimulation** with specific medications.
2. **Ultrasound follow-up** until the eggs reach a certain size.
3. **Egg retrieval** under light anesthesia.
4. **Injecting the eggs** with sperm.
5. **Monitoring the embryos** and transferring the best ones to the uterus.

## Does age affect ICSI success?

Yes. Success in ICSI depends on the wife's age:

- **Before 35:** a higher chance of success and better egg quality.
- **Between 35 and 40:** there is still a chance, but it is lower.
- **After 40:** the chances drop, and sometimes we consider freezing or other options depending on the case.
