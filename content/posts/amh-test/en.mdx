Can a single test tell us about your egg reserve? Yes.

## What is the AMH test?

The AMH test tells us about the **ovarian reserve**.

- **If the result is high:** that's good.
- **If the result is low:** it doesn't mean there's no hope, but we need to act quickly.

The test doesn't measure monthly ovulation, but it gives us an idea about the future.

## Weak ovulation vs. low reserve

Many people confuse ovulation with the reserve:

- **Ovulation** is the egg that grows each month.
- **The reserve** is the number of eggs still left in the ovary.

A woman can ovulate well every month while her reserve drops early. That's why the AMH test is very important alongside ovulation follow-up.
