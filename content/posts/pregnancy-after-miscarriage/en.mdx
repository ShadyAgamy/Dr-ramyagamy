The pregnancy came... and then it was gone? Can I get pregnant again?

## How long should we wait after a miscarriage?

After a natural miscarriage, we may wait **one or two cycles** before trying for a new pregnancy.

## If miscarriages repeat

If miscarriage happens again, what matters is finding the cause:

- We check the uterus.
- We check the hormones.
- We prepare the body for the next pregnancy.

## Emotional support

Emotional support is also very important.

Don't lose hope... every experience brings us closer to the dream.
