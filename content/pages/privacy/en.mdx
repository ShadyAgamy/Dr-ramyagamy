We respect your privacy. This page explains what data we collect when you use this website and how we use it.

## Data we collect

When you send the booking form, we receive only what you write:

- Your name
- Your phone number
- The clinic you choose
- Your preferred day
- A short note (optional)

**Please do not write any medical details in the form.** Your case will be discussed with the doctor directly.

## How we use your data

We use this data only to contact you and confirm your appointment by phone or WhatsApp. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing.

## Third-party services

- **Web3Forms:** booking form data is sent through Web3Forms to the clinic's email.
- **WhatsApp:** the WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp. The conversation is covered by WhatsApp's privacy policy.
- **Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics:** we may use Google tools to see how many people visit and which pages are most viewed, in general and without identifying you. These tools may use cookies.
- **Hosting:** the website is hosted on Netlify, which may keep technical logs such as IP addresses for security.
